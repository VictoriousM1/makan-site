How it works

From booking to a shareable link.

Plan

You send the address and size. We agree the rooms to cover, the start point and the tags you want.

Scan

We capture every room on site. Most spaces up to 300 m² take one to three hours.

Edit

We add your tags, prices, links, video and business card, and set the guided route.

Share

You receive one link for WhatsApp, listings and your website, and we can publish it on Google Maps.
