Interactive Google Maps

Turn your Google Maps listing into a tour people can walk into

Any scan we do for your business can be linked to its location on Google Maps. Your listing changes from a few still photos into an interactive tour, so people searching for you can walk around the place as if they were standing in it, before they decide to visit.

Ask about publishing your business →Get an estimate

How we add your tour to Google Maps

We scan your premises

One visit, usually one to three hours.

We add tags, prices and video

Your content, in Arabic and English.

We publish to your listing

The tour appears on your Google Maps business profile.

What appears inside the tour

Inside the tour we add whatever you want to explain: service prices, product prices and details, order and booking links, and videos that play inside the tour itself. The tour becomes a marketing and sales tool, not just pictures of the place.

It suits every place where things are shown or sold: showrooms and shops, clinics, restaurants, hotels, and sales and leasing offices.

A note on Google Maps publishing: Google allows tours of commercial and service businesses, such as clinics, restaurants, showrooms, hotels and offices. It does not allow private homes. A villa or apartment tour stays on a private link that you control.

Live example from Google Maps

This is how a high-resolution interior tour appears inside Google Maps: visitors move and look around 360° without leaving the map. The example is the Burj Khalifa in Dubai, captured by Google and published on Google Maps. It is not a Makan project.
