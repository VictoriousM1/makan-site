Questions

Common questions about this service

How many photos do I get?

Up to 30 high-resolution stills, chosen with you.

Can I get a vertical video?

Yes. We can cut a vertical version for Reels and Snapchat on request.

Is there a voiceover?

Not by default. We add your logo and simple captions. Voiceover can be quoted separately.

Can I order this without a tour?

Photos and video are taken during the scan visit, so they are ordered with a scan.
