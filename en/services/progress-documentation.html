Questions

Common questions about this service

How often should we scan?

Most projects scan at key milestones: before works, before closing walls and ceilings, and at handover. Large projects often add monthly visits.

Can owners view progress remotely?

Yes. Each visit has its own link, so owners and investors can see real progress without travelling to site.

Is this useful in a dispute?

A dated scan is a clear record of site conditions at a given time. It supports your records, but it is not a legal survey.

Do you need site access rules?

Yes. We follow the site safety rules and agree visit times with the site manager.
