Scan-to-BIM vs. traditional as-built survey

Both methods end with drawings or a model of the existing building. The difference is how the data is collected and what you can check afterwards.

Scan-to-BIMTraditional survey
Site timeShort: one scan visitLong: measuring by hand
Source data you keepFull point cloud and 3D tourField notes
Checking a drawing laterCompare with the point cloudReturn to site
Complex geometryCaptured as builtSimplified
Small, simple buildingsCan cost moreOften cheaper
DeliveryModel and drawingsDrawings
Our view

Which should you choose?

For complex, large or poorly documented buildings, Scan-to-BIM saves site time and gives you data you can check. For a small, simple building, a traditional survey can be the cheaper option.
