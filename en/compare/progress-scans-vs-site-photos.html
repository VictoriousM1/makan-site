Progress scans vs. site photos

Site photos are quick but scattered. A scan records the whole site at once, so months later you can still find what was behind a wall and where it was.

3D progress scansSite photos
CoverageEverything visible, every visitWhat someone chose to photograph
Finding a location laterWalk to it in 3DSearch through folders
MeasurementsYesNo
Speed on siteOne to three hours per visitMinutes
Sharing with ownersOne link per visitLarge photo sets
Our view

Which should you choose?

Keep taking site photos for daily notes. Add scans at key milestones, especially before walls and ceilings are closed.
