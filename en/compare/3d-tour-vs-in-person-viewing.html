3D tour vs. in-person viewing

A tour does not replace every visit. It filters them. People who arrive after seeing the tour already know the layout, so visits are shorter and more likely to end in a decision.

3D virtual tourIn-person viewing
AvailableAny time, from anywhereWhen someone can open the door
Number of viewersUnlimited, at the same timeOne group at a time
Sense of space and lightGoodBest
Touch and materialsNot possiblePossible
Sharing with family or partnersSend one linkEveryone must attend
Cost per viewingNothing after the scanStaff time and travel
Our view

Which should you choose?

Use the tour as the first viewing and keep in-person visits for serious buyers and tenants. You spend less time on visits that were never going to work.
