Phone scanning apps vs. a professional scan

Phone apps are useful for a quick sketch of one room. For a full property, a listing or design work, a professional scan is more complete, more consistent and ready to share.

Professional scanPhone app
Coverage of large spacesWhole buildingsOne or two rooms
Measurement consistencyHigh-accuracy across the spaceVaries with the phone and the user
Image qualityHigh resolution, even lightingPhone camera
Dollhouse, plans, tags, MapsIncludedLimited or none
Exports for CAD and BIME57, RCP, LASUsually basic formats
CostFrom the minimum project feeApp subscription
Our view

Which should you choose?

Use a phone app to check a room for yourself. Use a professional scan for anything clients will see or designers will build from.
